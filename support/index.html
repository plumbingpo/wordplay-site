Wordplay Support

Before contacting us, please read the FAQ for the quickest answers to your issues.

Do you offer a trial?

We offer a 7 day trial and you can get started with it by visiting our pricing page.

Can I get a discount?

The only prices we offer are available on our site and we don't offer private discounts. As you buy a plan that includes more words, you pay less money per word, so the best way to get a discount is by purchasing a higher tiered plan!

How much do enterprise plans cost?

Enterprise plans start at $1k per month. Select "I want more than 1m words/mo" in the form below and we'll get you set up.

My plan renewed, but I don't see more words in my account!

Our system runs on a 30-day rolling basis for words. All articles created in the last 30 days are counted against your usage. If you created an article 31 days ago, it no longer affects your usage.

If you're seeing another issue with your word count, reach out below and we will be happy to help!

Wordplay gave me a bad output, what do I do?

The best output comes from using single article mode or bulk mode so that you can define your subheadings. This gives the AI much more info on what you want your content to be about, especially with niche subjects.

When using AI mode, we recommend only using it for common, broad topics that can be answered in a variety of ways.

We do not give credit back on generated content that goes the wrong way, because we have hard costs on all words generated, so please be careful about how you're using the tool. The AI improves every day, so output quality keeps getting better over time.

What languages does Wordplay support?

English, Dutch, French, German, Italian, Polish, Spanish, Portuguese, Portuguese (Brazilian), Bulgarian, Czech, Danish, Greek, Hungarian, Lithuanian, Latvian, Romanian, Slovak, Slovenian, Swedish, and Finnish.

Need help writing articles?

The quality of your articles depends on the quality you put into Wordplay. Give it specific, descriptive titles and define your subheadings in single article or bulk mode for the best results.
