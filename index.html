FAQ section

Your questions, answered.

Does Google penalize websites that write blogs with an AI writer?

This topic is widely debated and quite inconclusive. From our experience, we have done SEO testing for multiple websites using AI content and actually seen rankings increase. This may be because we were careful to fact-check the content and fix errors before publishing.

Google looks for content that is well-written, accurate, and relevant to the topic of the page. As long as content written with an AI writer meets those standards, it should not be penalized.

Does Wordplay write the entire article for you?

Yes, and there are multiple ways to use Wordplay:

  • Guided Mode: Start with a keyword and we take you through selecting your title, introduction, and subsections. Wordplay then writes the full article.
  • Title Mode: Give Wordplay a descriptive title and choose how long you want the article (up to ~5,000 words). Works in bulk.
  • Outline Mode: Give Wordplay your outline and we write the article.
  • Bulk FAQ Mode: Input a list of questions and get detailed answers for each.
  • Bulk CSV Mode: Upload a CSV of outlines and create content in bulk.
  • Topic Mode: Type any topic and get content about it.
Will AI writing tools replace human writers?

What AI writing tools can do is supercharge human writing. An AI writer gets the ideas from your head onto the page quicker, and helps you brainstorm. If you use that to your advantage, you will be a faster content writer than ever before.

Can Wordplay generate content in multiple languages?

Yes, Wordplay writes in 20+ languages including English, Dutch, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and many more.

How accurate is Wordplay's grammar and spelling?

Wordplay articles are typically 95-100% grammatically correct. As with any AI tool, you may want to run the content through a plagiarism checker before publishing.

Wordplay quickly creates titles, outlines, and fresh content focused on the keywords and phrases your audience is searching for, so you can publish more topically relevant content and build authority faster. It works alongside your existing keyword and optimization tools.
